Tips

  • Dry-fit each corner pair before nailing. Walls are rarely exactly 90°, so a 44°/46° split may close a gap better than 45°/45°.
  • Nail into the baseboard, not the floor, so floating floors can still move.
  • Use small mitered returns at the dishwasher and stove ends instead of square cuts. They look cleaner and won't snag a mop.
  • At the sliding door, leave the end a little short so it doesn't interfere with the door track.
  • Fit piece 9 (4") before piece 8 (23"), then cut the 23" to fit.
  • Piece 11 meets the existing dining room quarter round. Miter it to the old piece, or trim the old piece back slightly for a clean joint.
  • PVC cuts cleanly with a fine-tooth (60T or more) miter saw blade. Cut slowly so the edges don't melt or chip.
  • PVC expands and contracts with temperature more than wood. Nail about every 12", and glue outside-corner miters with PVC trim adhesive or CA glue so they don't open up.
  • Fill nail holes with white caulk (no sanding or painting needed), and wipe it smooth with a damp finger.
